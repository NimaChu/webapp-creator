# Visual Directions

Choose one direction from the task, then adapt it to the subject. Do not ask the user to select a starter unless multiple directions would materially change the result.

## Workbench

Use for repeated utilities, converters, inspectors, and editors.

- Density: compact.
- Structure: controls and output visible together.
- Emphasis: speed, keyboard use, and clear state.
- Signature: a subject-specific inspector, preview, or transformation view.

## Analytics

Use for dashboards and reports.

- Density: balanced to compact.
- Structure: metrics, trends, exceptions, then detail.
- Emphasis: comparison and decision support.
- Signature: a meaningful threshold, variance, or relationship view.

## Guided

Use for configuration, diagnosis, review, and multi-step forms.

- Density: balanced.
- Structure: progress, current step, validation, review.
- Emphasis: confidence about what happens next.
- Signature: a task-specific summary or branching decision.

## Knowledge

Use for guides, meeting material, FAQs, and organized reference content.

- Density: readable.
- Structure: search or navigation beside scannable content.
- Emphasis: retrieval and comprehension.
- Signature: a useful taxonomy, timeline, glossary, or evidence treatment.

## Playful

Use for games and creative interactions.

- Density: controlled by the playfield.
- Structure: score/status, playfield, controls, result.
- Emphasis: the core loop and immediate feedback.
- Signature: one memorable mechanic or visual response.

## Direction Contract

Before coding, specify:

- Subject and real vocabulary.
- Direction and density.
- Light, dark, or system-aware scheme.
- Four to six named colors.
- Display, body, and data type roles.
- Information structure in one sentence.
- One useful treatment the app will be remembered by.

If the same design would fit an unrelated app after changing only the title, make the structure or signature more specific.

Avoid generic AI styling, decorative gradients, oversized rounding, nested cards, excessive centering, and animation without informational value.
